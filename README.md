# Logistics KPI Performance Review

> ⚠️ **ALL DATA IN THIS PROJECT IS SYNTHETIC.** It was generated by `src/generate_data.py` with seed 42 for an
> internship evaluation. It is **not real company data** — no number in this repository describes a real business,
> real customers, or real money. The fictional company, its hubs, routes, vehicles and warehouses do not exist.
> Do not present any finding here as real-world performance.

## Scenario

A fictional Kolkata-based cold-chain distributor operating across **12 hubs** in eastern India (West Bengal, Assam,
Bihar, Jharkhand, Odisha), with **60 routes**, **180 vehicles** and **24 warehouses**, moving five product
categories (Frozen, Chilled, Ambient, Dairy, Pharma). The review covers the calendar year **2025 only**
(1 January – 31 December 2025), so there is **no true year-over-year comparison** — H1 vs H2 2025 is used as the
prior-period comparison and is labelled as such everywhere.

**Author:** Khushi Tomar — Yuva Intern, Logistics Data Analytics Specialist Internship (evaluation project).
Final-year B.Tech, CSE (AI & ML).

## Objectives

1. Build a clean, reconciled analytical dataset from messy synthetic source files (duplicates, mixed hub spellings,
   mixed timestamp formats, invalid costs, orphan scan events, out-of-order scans, negative dwell pairs).
2. Model it as a star schema with one trusted analysis table (one row per shipment).
3. Measure three KPIs — **on-time delivery %**, **cost per shipment (INR)**, **hub dwell time (hours)** — with
   documented definitions and segment breakdowns (month, hub, route, warehouse, category, vehicle type).
4. Find where lateness concentrates, rank performers, and quantify the modelled business impact of bringing
   bottom-quartile segments up to the network median.
5. Deliver a reproducible analysis: executable notebook, Power BI documentation (measures + build guide), and
   charts — all verified by an independent validation pass (**38/38 checks PASS, 0% variance**).

**Where the problem is:**
- **Destination hub dominates.** Nine hubs sit at ~83–84% OTD; three collapse — **Guwahati 53.95%**,

- **Monthly OTD is flat** (75.72% in April to 77.04% in May) — no seasonal swing, no trend. The problem is
  structural (hubs/routes), not seasonal.
- **Category:** Pharma is best on OTD (**84.59%**) but most expensive (₹7,226.40/shipment); Frozen is worst
  (**72.06%**, 33.1% of all late deliveries) at ₹4,466.98. Vehicle type is flat (76.24–76.64% across all five

- **Concentration:** 39 of 61 routes account for 80% of late deliveries (Pareto); the latest 10% of eligible

**Modelled business impact** (realistic case — bottom-quartile segments converge to the network median;
modelled estimate on synthetic data, **not a forecast**): **9,706 late deliveries avoided**,
**₹31,13,833 saving**, **4,295.8 excess dwell hours recovered**.
Conservative (50% of gap closed): 4,853 / ₹15,56,916 / 2,147.9 h. Optimistic (+10% efficiency on cost):

## KPI definitions

| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| **On-time delivery (OTD) %** | On-time eligible deliveries ÷ eligible deliveries × 100. Eligible = status Delivered/Delayed **and** promised & actual timestamps both valid. Cancelled / Returned / In-Transit shipments never completed delivery and are excluded from the denominator. |
| **Late deliveries** | Eligible shipments with actual delivery after the promised time. |
| **Cost per shipment (₹)** | Sum of valid shipment costs ÷ count of valid costs. Negative, zero or missing costs are flagged `cost_valid=False` and excluded from the denominator; the shipment row is kept. |
| **Avg destination dwell (h)** | Mean of (Delivered − Arrival) over valid, non-negative scan pairs. Origin dwell = (Departure − Loading). Combined = row-wise mean of the two. Negative pairs are flagged invalid and excluded, not corrected. |
| **MoM change** | Month-over-month: OTD in percentage points; cost and dwell in %. |
| **Prior-period comparison** | H1 2025 vs H2 2025. The data covers 2025 only, so no year-over-year exists. |

## Recommendations

1. **Corridor recovery — Guwahati / Malda / Ranchi.** Fix these three corridors and the 15 bottom-quartile routes
   (OTD ≤ 73.69%, median route OTD 80.06%) before any network-wide programme. Modelled effect: 9,706 late
   deliveries avoided. Start with routes RT-006, RT-017, RT-028 and RT-005.
2. **Destination-hub dwell reduction.** Destination dwell (15.49 h) is ~6× origin dwell (2.50 h) and flat across
   warehouses and months, so this is a destination-process problem (unloading / dock scheduling / handover), not a
   local failure. Bottom-quartile warehouses converging to the median recovers 4,295.8 excess hours. Diagnose
   first at WH-CTC-1 (15.95 h, the highest). Report dwell savings in **hours** — do not convert to rupees.
3. **Frozen-category reliability + bottom-quartile route cost control.** Give Frozen (72.06% OTD, 33.1% of late
   deliveries) a dedicated cold-chain review on the weak corridors. Separately, bring the 15 most expensive routes
   (≥ ₹4,122.72/shipment) to the median route cost (₹4,099.68) for a modelled ₹31,13,833 saving. Do **not**
   target Pharma's ₹7,226.40/shipment as a cost problem — it is the best OTD category and its cost reflects its
   service requirements.

## Data cleaning — what was done (rules C1–C9)

| Rule | Action | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| C1 Dedupe | Keep the most complete record per `shipment_id` | 4,500 duplicate copies removed |
| C2 Hub crosswalk | Map 60 raw hub spellings → 12 canonical ids via `hub_crosswalk.csv` | 91,486 origin / 92,027 destination rows standardised |
| C3 Timestamps | `pd.to_datetime(..., format='mixed', utc=True)`; naive stamps assumed UTC; unparseable → NaT, excluded from OTD | 2,323 promised / 2,219 actual unparseable (post-dedupe) |
| C4 Cost validation | Negative / zero / missing cost → `cost_valid=False`, excluded from cost KPI | 2,439 negative, 1,156 zero, 2,920 missing (post-dedupe) |
| C5 Missing values | Never imputed; missing category/vehicle → `Unknown` / `UNKNOWN` member | 857 category, 1,429 vehicle_id |
| C6 Invalid references | Unknown `route_id` / `vehicle_id` → `UNKNOWN` member | 1,521 route, 1,543 vehicle |
| C7 Orphan scans | Scans with no matching shipment removed | 6,000 removed; 0 remain |
| C8 Re-sequencing | Scans sorted by UTC timestamp per shipment; `scan_sequence` re-assigned | 29,287 events in 20,914 shipments; 0 out-of-order remain |
| C9 Negative dwell | Flagged `dwell_valid=False`, excluded from dwell KPI | 2,885 destination / 18 origin (post-dedupe) |

Full detail: `outputs/cleaning_logs/cleaning_log.csv` (19 issue types) and
`outputs/kpi_reports/data_quality_report.md`. Note that profiling counts (e.g. 2,474 negative costs) are on the
raw files including duplicates, while cleaning-log counts (2,439) are post-dedupe — both are correct at their stage.

## Limitations

- **Synthetic data.** All findings are about generated data (seed 42). Nothing here generalises to a real
  logistics operation, and the business-impact figures are modelled estimates, not forecasts of real savings.
  The improvement model assumes bottom-quartile segments converge to the network median, with no causal mechanism,
  no behaviour change, no capacity constraints and no implementation costs.
- **No true year-over-year.** Data covers 2025 only; H1 vs H2 2025 is the prior-period comparison.
- **Naive timestamps assumed UTC.** Source stamps were IST/UTC mixed and naive stamps carry no offset (rule C3);
  treating them as UTC keeps chronological order consistent, but absolute clock times for naive stamps may shift.
- **No verified per-hour handling cost**, so dwell savings are reported in hours only, never converted to rupees.
- **Dwell is derived from scan pairs** (Departure−Loading, Delivered−Arrival); negative pairs are flagged and
  excluded, not corrected.
- **Costs are synthetic magnitudes** with no fuel / toll / labour split — cost analysis is limited to
  per-shipment averages by segment.
- **Validation:** `outputs/validation_report.md` — 38/38 checks PASS, 0% variance on every reconciled figure
  (row counts, OTD, cost, dwell, segment sums, referential integrity). The notebook independently recomputes the
  headline KPIs and business-impact model and asserts them against `kpi_summary.json`.
